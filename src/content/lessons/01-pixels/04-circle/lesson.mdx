`distance(uv, center)` measures how far each pixel is from the center.

`smoothstep` turns that distance into a soft change: 1 (white) near the center, 0 (black) further out.
Its first two numbers say where the edge starts and where it ends.
The further apart they are, the blurrier the edge.

**Your turn:** drag the center on the image, then set the size and the blur of the edge.
