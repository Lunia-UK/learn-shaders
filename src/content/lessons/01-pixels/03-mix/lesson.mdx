`mix(a, b, t)` blends two colors.
When `t` is 0 you get `a`. When `t` is 1 you get `b`. In between, you get a blend of both.

Here `t` is `uv.x`, so the blend goes from left to right: a gradient.

**Your turn:** the target fades from the bottom to the top, toward another purple.
To change the direction, write line 4 yourself. Then adjust color `b`.
